B. Prathik Kumar — I go by Prax. I design digital products and visual identities, and I study Electronics & Communication Engineering at Anurag University, Hyderabad.

The two sides feed each other more than people expect — the same attention that goes into routing a clean signal path goes into setting a type scale or laying out a checkout flow. I care about systems holding together under real constraints, whether that's an FPGA timing budget or a design system that has to survive three different editorial teams.

Currently a UI/UX Design Intern at FlyRank AI, designing interfaces and prototyping in Figma for AI-powered products. Previously a Junior Graphic Designer Intern at SuPraZo Technologies. Outside of coursework and internships, I lead design and strategy for a few student organizations at Anurag University.
