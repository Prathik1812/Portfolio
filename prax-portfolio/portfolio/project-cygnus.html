Cygnus

Identity for the ECE department's Cygnus club — a black swan mark built to anchor a newsletter that's now run for three consecutive years.

ClientDept. of ECE, AU
DomainClub Identity
RoleGraphic Designer
Year2022–2024

The Mark

The Cygnus constellation is named for a swan — so the mark literally draws one, rising from the department's ECE crest, paired with a hand-cut ransom-note wordmark that gives the otherwise formal symbol some personality.

That contrast — a formal emblem against a scrappy, collaged wordmark — became the visual rule for everything the club puts out under the Cygnus name.

Cygnus Times

Twice a year, the mark anchors a new cover for Cygnus Times, the department newsletter — each issue takes a different photographic mood (circuitry, desk objects, macro shots) while keeping the same masthead, crest placement and date block, so the series reads as one publication even as the imagery changes.

Outcome

A student club identity that's
outlasted three editorial teams.
