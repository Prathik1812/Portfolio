Attack on Titan

A character selection interface concept designed to feel tactical and cinematic — where military HUD structure meets anime typography and character-driven storytelling.

TypeGame Interface
DomainConcept / Fan Design
RoleUI Designer
Year2026

Problem & Goal

Most anime game menus feel generic and lack narrative intensity — character selection screens often present information passively instead of emotionally.

The goal was a character selection interface that feels tactical and militaristic, highlights each character's personality, blends typography with anime visuals, and creates anticipation before gameplay even begins.

Navigation & System

Character switching runs on a vertical arrow system that mimics dossier browsing — each hover subtly glows and shifts emphasis to the active character's insignia, making selection feel deliberate rather than instant.

Every screen follows the same modular structure: character render, oversized symbolic kanji in the background, name and age metadata, a narrative description, an ability preview card, and faction insignia — consistent enough to scale to a full cast, distinct enough per character.

Military HUD Kanji Symbolism Tactical Color Coding Modular Grid

Typography & Color

A geometric techno display face carries names — dominant and futuristic — paired down to a minimal tactical label for age and a compact narrative block, keeping the information hierarchy readable at a glance.

Color is assigned per character rather than fixed: black for tension and depth, gold for hierarchy, crimson or violet for individual identity, white for readability — so the palette itself becomes part of each character's signature.

Outcome

A selection screen that feels
like entering the world
before gameplay begins.
