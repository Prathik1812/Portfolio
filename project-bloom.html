Bloom & Co.

A calm, premium shopping journey for floral gifting — designed end to end from product discovery through checkout, delivery tracking and account management.

PlatformiOS / Android / Web
DomainFloral Commerce
RoleProduct Designer (UX+UI)
Year2026

The Brief

Most floral delivery apps either bury the product behind cluttered filters or lean so hard into decoration that the actual bouquet gets lost. Bloom & Co. needed a browsing experience that felt as calm and considered as the product itself — flowers as a gift, not just inventory.

The scope covered the full commerce loop: discovery, product detail and customization, cart, checkout, delivery tracking, order history, and account management — built as one continuous system rather than disconnected screens.

User Flow

The flow was mapped as seven deliberate steps, each with a single job: view the product, customize size and add-ons, add to cart, checkout, select delivery, pay, and track the order home.

01 View Product 02 Customize 03 Add to Cart 04 Checkout 05 Select Delivery 06 Payment 07 Track Order

Product Discovery & Detail

The browsing experience centers on bouquet photography with minimal distraction — soft cards with rounded corners and neutral backgrounds that let the arrangement, size and tone speak for themselves.

On the product page, customization is simplified into clear selectable chips instead of dropdowns — size, add-ons and reviews stay visible without asking the user to dig for them.

Cart, Checkout & Tracking

Payment methods (UPI, card, net banking) are presented as equal, clearly-labelled options rather than a biased default — and once an order is placed, a dedicated tracking screen shows delivery progress as a simple linear timeline: placed, dispatched, out for delivery, delivered.

Outcome

A commerce system calm enough
to match what it's selling.
